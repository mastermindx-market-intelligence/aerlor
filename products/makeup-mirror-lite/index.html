THE DESIGN INTENTION

Put the finishing touch in the right light.

A beautiful room can still leave a guest searching for a mirror near the light. Mirror Lite puts that everyday need at the centre of the design brief: a useful position, a considered footprint and a clear view before the day begins.

Imagined for
Guestroom desks, compact vanities and dressing areas
