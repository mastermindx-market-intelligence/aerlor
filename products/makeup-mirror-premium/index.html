THE DESIGN INTENTION

A vanity moment worth making room for.

Give the vanity the same point of view as the rest of the suite. Mirror Premium explores a generous circular form and a refined metal finish, bringing the look of the room and the reality of getting ready into one composition.

Imagined for
Suites, premium dressing tables, bridal preparation rooms and spa-adjacent spaces

MAKE IT WORK IN YOUR ROOMS

Consider the placement.
Then the whole program.

Treat it as part of a complete dressing station, not a decorative object placed wherever space remains.

Before the sample

Confirm the destination market, proposed room location and the exact technical evidence you need.

During evaluation

Review the actual unit with engineering and housekeeping. Gather guest feedback without substituting it for safety approval.

Before rollout

Agree the final model, written quote, kit contents, lead time and service terms.
