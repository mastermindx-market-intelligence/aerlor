THE DESIGN INTENTION

The detail that earns a second look.

Luxury does not stop at the edge of the bathroom shelf. Hairdryer Premium is imagined as an object your guest is pleased to find, not an appliance they wish they had packed. A refined material direction gives the everyday ritual a more deliberate place in the room.
