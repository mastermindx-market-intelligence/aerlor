THE DESIGN INTENTION

A better beginning to every day.

The first meeting. The unhurried breakfast. The last look before dinner. A hairdryer belongs to all of those moments. Hairdryer Lite is our everyday hospitality expression: a pared-back form, a soft-matte finish direction and a product brief focused on simple, comfortable use.

Imagined for
Guestrooms, serviced apartments and thoughtful select-service stays
