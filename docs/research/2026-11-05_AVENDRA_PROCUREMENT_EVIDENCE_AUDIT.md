**As of 2026-11-05 | Public-safe research | No authenticated Avendra price or supplier award observed**

## Executive finding

**Pursue Avendra as a conditional distribution option, not a launch dependency.** AERLOR's initial route remains funded hotel openings, renovations, qualified owner/operators and OS&E agents. The relevant Avendra, Guest Supply and AAHOA/Folio ecosystem could eventually lower purchasing friction, but supplier inquiry, GPO award, distributor stocking, hotel brand-standard approval, member catalog visibility and purchase orders are distinct steps.

This paper audits the earlier Avendra price findings against publicly accessible first-party sources and existing AERLOR company research. It is **not** an audit of the later expanded Deep Research report: that full report was not available in the connected document repositories at preparation. If supplied, reconcile its exact claims and source register before promoting any new fee, contact, SKU or award conclusion.

| Path | Buyer and commercial event | AERLOR revenue concept | Missing contract evidence |

AAHOA's launch release describes the Folio software and Avendra sourcing arrangement, **not** a universal contract price, a specific commission or automatic demand [S3]. ## 4. Why an average discount claim cannot price this product

A reported marketplace saving percentage is not a hairdryer-specific manufacturer's wholesale discount. Separate: (i) seller's public list; (ii) hotel contracted delivered price; (iii) distributor purchase price; (iv) distributor gross margin; (v) GPO administrative fees/allowances; (vi) member rebates; (vii) promotional/placement fees; (viii) freight, returns, chargebacks and payment terms.

For stocked distribution, if hotel sellout is H and distributor gross margin is d, manufacturer sell-in is **S = H × (1 − d)**. Supplier-side fees, when actually contractually applicable, are calculated on their stated bases; they are not automatically a second copy of the distributor margin. Manufacturer contribution = realized supplier net − landed cost − variable service, fulfillment and acquisition costs. **No Avendra-specific numeric fee, discount or margin is established by the public sources reviewed.**

## 5. Competitive implication for AERLOR

AERLOR's prelaunch hotel price concepts cannot win merely by appearing less expensive than Dyson. Hotels often compare the new purchase against a working $25–$50 incumbent and against other high-speed or hospitality-specific brands. Existing AERLOR research documents Laifen's bulk route, Vanity Group WAVES, JVD and other premium products; a brushless motor, metal housing or attractive silhouette is not sufficient differentiation. See [AERLOR competition matrix](2026-11-04_PRODUCT_COMPETITION.md).

A hotel buying at a hypothetical $79 versus a $29 public commodity benchmark faces **$50 incremental purchase cost per room**: 100 rooms $5,000; 250 rooms $12,500; 500 rooms $25,000 before tax, freight, installation and spares. This is an optional amenity investment; no ADR lift, guest satisfaction ROI, failure-rate advantage or energy payback has been measured. A credible offer needs independent performance evidence, exact-model electrical suitability, local service and a **funded** refresh/OS&E window.

## 6. Research falsifiers before partnership pursuit

1. **Category:** Who actually owns *guestroom hair dryers* at Avendra, not spa salon products? Is a 2027 RFP or off-cycle route open?
2. **Award vs activation:** Does acceptance mean supplier registration, category award, hotel catalog visibility, marketing support or enforceable purchase orders?
3. **Member prices:** Can an authorized buyer show the exact model/quantity/delivered price for Conair, higher-end incumbents and Dyson (if present)? Do not ask for unauthorized account access.
4. **Stock vs drop-ship:** Who takes title, holds inventory, handles RMA, pays freight and invoices the property?
5. **Economic fees:** What is each fee/rebate's basis, trigger, timing, pass-through, tax treatment and stacking order?
6. **Brand standards:** Which properties can use a new electrical appliance SKU, and who can authorize an alternate?
7. **Demand:** How many category units did eligible members actually buy in the previous 12 months, and which named buyers have a funded upcoming project?
8. **Readiness:** What exact 120V/60Hz certification, immersion protection, warranty, insurance, QA, import and service requirements apply?
