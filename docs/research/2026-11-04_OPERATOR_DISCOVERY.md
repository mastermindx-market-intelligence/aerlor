# AERLOR | Multi-Property Owner and Operator Discovery Map
**2026-11-04 | Public-first-party evidence | Not a qualified sales pipeline**

This map lists **publicly described hotel operators** with potential to aggregate guestroom equipment decisions. It does not claim anyone has expressed interest, that hotels need dryers, or that every property has centrally controlled purchasing. Detailed private contacts, proposal prices, outreach histories and contract negotiations belong in an access-controlled CRM, not this public repository.

## Why operators matter

A successful 200-room property rollout is a finite equipment order. Selling to a shared owner/operator who can assess and repeat an approved specification across more properties may reduce the number of separate prospecting cycles. **An operator's hotel count is not a unit forecast:** property ownership, franchise standards, independent owners and capital approval differ, and individual hotels may opt out.

## Qualification milestones (do before sending a priced proposal)

1. Find the actual portfolio subset with discretionary OS&E purchasing and upcoming openings/renovations.
2. Confirm the named **commercial** buyer, legal buying entity, brand approvals, operational evaluator and property owner budget.
3. Get current model and buyer alternatives (including inexpensive high-speed units) and their **actual** delivered prices.
4. Ask whether the business is an *installed upgrade*, a funded opening specification, suites-only, or replacement-on-failure.
5. Verify whether a successful paid pilot can expand to named additional properties and by what approval mechanism.
6. Record exact legal and safety requirements for intended market and product classification before deploying samples.

## Account prioritization logic

Do not rank purely by room count. Prefer, in order:
- A funded opening or upgrade project **with purchasing access**.
- An independent operator with design-led brand fit and an authorized OS&E decision-maker.
- A management group where multiple named properties share a buyer and standards.
- Larger branded/franchised portfolios only after alternate-product approvals are demonstrably feasible.
- Passive GPO listings without funded project activation do not qualify.

### First interview asks
- **Lark/Aparium:** What in-room appliance upgrades are supported centrally, and could a paid proof-of-concept cover 20–30 rooms?
- **Germain/InnVest:** Which model/brand standards and Canadian approvals determine selection? Are any 2027–28 budgets being built?
- **Northland project:** Who has the Sutton Place Kelowna pre-opening OS&E specification and at what date are hairdryers frozen?
- **Atlific:** Is there a particular independent hotel and one affiliated property willing to discuss their actual buying process?
