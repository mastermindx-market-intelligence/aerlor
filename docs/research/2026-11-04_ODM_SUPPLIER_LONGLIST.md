# AERLOR | Hairdryer ODM Supplier Longlist
**Desk research 2026-11-04 | NO suppliers contacted, vetted, contracted or approved**

## Screening premise

Factory websites are **supplier self-descriptions**. Listing a plant size, certificate logo, MOQ, motor rating or volume claim is not independent proof of manufacturing control, quality, product safety or genuine legal ownership. This list authorizes **verification and requests for evidence**, not factory selection.

AERLOR needs an *independently designed* product that satisfies **the actual destination market** (US 120 V/60 Hz and/or applicable Canadian acceptance), is safe for hotel use, does not infringe protected ornamental designs and can survive real procurement/warranty economics. Ask for a sample and exact SKU, not a catalog of thousands of unqualified models.

### High-priority audit sequence (before any paid order)

1. Cross-check Chinese legal business license and official full company name, registered site, beneficial ownership and factory site. Request live dated video walkthrough; obtain third-party audit if candidate survives.
2. Obtain **exact model number**, photos/video of stocked SKU, materials and nameplate. Require the certificate **holder and certificate/listing number**; independently confirm with issuing lab's official registry.
3. Request electrical safety dossier for intended use, including CPSC integral immersion protection in the US, market-specific plug/cord, thermostat/overload, housing behavior, accessible filter and fault protection.
4. Ask for anonymous, permissioned manufacturing references and shipment/claim history by exact model. No unapproved disclosure of other customers.
5. Compare factory quotation under the *same fixed specification* for samples, 100 units, 500, 1,000 and 5,000 units, with declared Incoterms, lead times, tooling, modifications and payment protections. Put price/quote in a **restricted** sourcing folder, not public GitHub.
6. Require no unauthorized component substitutions, lot traceability, independent pre-shipment inspection and a binding commercial-use warranty / recall procedure.
7. Counsel-led patent/design FTO and importer/broker review are independent requirements; factory assurances are not legal clearance.
8. Re-evaluate ranked candidate selection after receiving evidence; **do not assume A-rated research prospects are good production partners**.

### RFQ question for all prospects

> 'Please identify one existing original-design North America-relevant high-speed hairdryer SKU and provide the legal manufacturer, current traceable safety certification, full rated 120 V/60 Hz characteristics, independent test report numbers, commercial warranty, production case/quality history and sample/MOQ pricing, with the evidence holder named. We will only evaluate a factory whose paperwork matches the specific production BOM.'

This is a **draft question**, not a message sent or a supplier's response.

## Research limitations

- No Chinese national corporate-registry checks or in-person factory audits were completed.
- Claims of ETL / UL859 are unverified. A certificate for *another model* or safety-mark misuse is not acceptable.
- All listed sites might present marketing-optimized descriptions. Company founding dates, output, delivery lead times, customer counts, product originality and warranties need independent evidence.
- Quoted consumer or 1688 prices are not full US/Canadian landed commercial cost; no prices were obtained from these counterparties.
- The 100–110 V 'dual voltage US' claim must not be accepted at face value for the US 120 V /60 Hz application.
- No prospective supplier is exclusive, approved, contracted or known to satisfy requirements.
