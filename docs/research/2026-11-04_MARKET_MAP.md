# AERLOR | Hospitality Market & Channel Evidence Map
**Research snapshot: 2026-11-04 (US/Canada first; international expansion later). Public sources only.**

Evidence is marked **Documented** (direct original issuer/source), **Inference** (reasonable strategic synthesis), or **Unverified** (requires independent data). A vendor site establishes the vendor's offer, not its sales volume or product superiority.

## Executive conclusion

There is an observable market for higher-end hotel dryers and loanable beauty tools, **but no evidence yet of a vacant category or a guaranteed high-conversion purchasing opportunity**. The actual wedge must combine documented product performance, hotel-compatible operations, credible brand experience and a repeatable decision-maker/channel.

**Key change:** the business is not built by waiting for legacy dryers to break. It needs a funded room-experience upgrade, new-property spec, or existing distributor channel with actual orders.

### Named buyer archetypes to identify, in order
1. Owner/operator purchasing lead **with a budget and multiple properties**; operational rooms/housekeeping and engineering co-sponsors.
2. Independent hotel program/category development lead who can organize named paid pilot properties.
3. OS&E opening/renovation project purchasing lead with an active guestroom equipment specification.
4. Existing amenities manufacturer/distributor considering a complementary appliance range.
5. A stocking hospitality distributor with demonstrated demand and explicit launch economics.
6. Hotel chain/GPO category manager for approved sourcing **after** product readiness and reference sales.

## Customer problem: what is and is not proven

- **Documented:** hotel operators have intentionally deployed premium dryers and offered styling tools; QT and Nobu supply examples.
- **Documented:** Hyatt historically undertook a guest-listening initiative that informed a lending service including curling tools.
- **Hypothesis:** a materially larger market of 4–5-star hotels would pay for upgrading functioning dryers at a relatively low per-room spend.
- **Hypothesis:** a new North American-positioned brand would receive better acceptance than Laifen at similar net cost.
- **Hypothesis:** women travelers as a group value particular tools; validate with diverse actual users, hair types and routines. Do not infer universal preference or promise a proven occupancy/ADR lift.

The demand experiment is a real buyer agreeing to a funded test and then placing an order at a stated net price—not a favorable showroom reaction.

## Economics: model correctly, avoid public disclosure of sensitive targets

A valid transaction model must distinguish:
- **Hotel purchase price** (direct), **distributor sell-in price**, **distributor gross margin**, **GPO/contracted rebates**, **sales commissions**, and **consumer retail**. Do not stack every intermediary margin blindly.
- Product ex-works cost vs **complete landed cost** (freight, classification, duties, customs processing, damage, packaging, storage and quality).
- Warranty reserve and outbound replacement freight, on-site loss, returns, installation and customer service.
- Recurring replacement orders vs one-time whole-property rollouts; cash tied up in stock and 30–90-day receivables.
- Genuine consumer pricing and claims substantiation, not arbitrary MSRP anchoring.

This public repository intentionally excludes internal buyer-specific bids, supplier quotes, and contribution models. Keep the numerical model in an approved restricted location.

## Regulatory, trade, IP and claim issues

1. **US safety:** CPSC states that hand-supported dryers lacking integral immersion protection are substantial product hazards under 16 CFR 1120; importantly, this particular 15(j) rule does not automatically trigger a separate CPSA §14 certification requirement. Product-market lab review, retailer/hotel specifications and other applicable requirements still need confirmation. Source: https://www.cpsc.gov/FAQ/Hand-Supported-Hair-Dryers
2. **Freedom to operate:** Dyson publishes patent marking lists and Laifen has active US ornamental design records. That a general high-speed technology exists does not authorize copying a protected housing or patented mechanism. Sources: https://www.dyson.com/inside-dyson/patents ; https://patents.google.com/patent/USD954345S1/en
3. **US imports:** likely starting point for stand-alone hair dryer classification is HTS 8516.31; total import charges depend on origin, entry date, applicable Chapter 99 measures, product facts, exclusions and logistics. Public tariff aggregators currently disagree substantially on added duties. **Obtain a broker-confirmed landed-cost calculation for the exact SKU and arrival date; never use a single third-party calculator as a quote.** Sources: https://hts.usitc.gov/ ; https://www.usitc.gov/harmonized_tariff_information ; https://www.ustr.gov/issue-areas/enforcement/section-301-investigations/search
4. **Consumer MSRP and origin:** genuine and supportable retail pricing, accurate country-of-origin representation, no untested claims about hair damage, drying time, sound or quality; reserve claims review for final product. Sources: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-233/section-233.3 ; https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-made-usa-standard
