# AERLOR | Funded Hotel Openings and OS&E Specification Windows
**As of 2026-11-04 | Public-source preliminary research, NOT a qualified order pipeline**

## Why this matters

Upgrading functioning hotel dryers is an optional capex/OS&E discussion. A new build, refurbishment, conversion or pre-opening project **already has a specification and purchasing workflow**. AERLOR should ask to be evaluated *at that workflow*, not pretend every announcement is a sales lead.

[Lodging Econometrics (Q2 2026)](https://lodgingeconometrics.com/us-hotel-construction-pipeline-q2-2026/) reported:
- 2027 expected openings: **738 properties / 78,909 rooms** (all US chain-scale segments).
- 2028 expected openings: **832 properties / 88,321 rooms**.
- Luxury pipeline: **103 projects / 25,496 rooms**; upper-upscale **367 projects / 65,021 rooms** (projects at all stages, not 2027/28 deliveries).
- Conversions and renovations together: **2,097 projects / 255,834 rooms**.
- These counts **overlap categories/time windows**, and cannot be summed into an addressable market. They are pipeline expectations, not purchasing commitments or proof that hairdryers will be included in any particular tender.

**Implication:** building a repeatable path to the OS&E specification may matter more than replacing installed legacy dryers. The highest priority is learning when the **guestroom electrical appliance specification freezes**, not contacting a hotel's opening GM just before launch.

## Important exclusion: not every announced key is an AERLOR sale

Some developments mix **hotel, residential and private condominium** accommodation. Others are brand-mandated, where the owner cannot freely swap out electrical products. Opening dates slip, rooms change, and FF&E/OS&E specs may be locked many months ahead. Rankings above are **qualification hypotheses**, not reliable demand estimates.

**Room opportunity sizing:** for a confirmed hotel-controlled room count R, a project might need approximately R dryers plus a negotiated spare allowance if hairdryers are purchased as a room standard. Only after an actual owner/category decision should that become a dollar-valued weighted forecast.

## Standard approach to a funded opening

1. Confirm the project is still active and named owner / operator / brand / appointed purchasing agent.
2. Request the **exact electrical OS&E specification** and whether an approved substitute can be considered.
3. Confirm *specification freeze* and anticipated RFQ, mockup, PO and delivery dates.
4. Identify how the project measures performance, packaging, warranties, insurance and delivery risk.
5. Submit a **fully qualified exact model** only when product tests, approvals and logistics are real.
6. Ask for a small explicit *paid* sample/mockup order and a decision date, not an indefinite free sample.
7. Track a purchase order separately from a catalog listing or verbal agreement.

## ICP and first geographically compact cluster

**Canada West** is a useful buyer-discovery cluster (Kelowna, Victoria, Northland and independent upscale properties) **if** Canadian product readiness is feasible. This is not a recommendation to ship US appliances into Canada without approval.

**US Southeast** presents a separate path through documented project procurement firms and Raines/Concord networks. Do not pursue both as stocked markets before test/approvals and supply economics are established.
