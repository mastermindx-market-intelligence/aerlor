## 1. Fixed evaluation envelope

- Evaluate one existing, independently designed, corded high-speed dryer platform per supplier for **120 V / 60 Hz US use**. This is AERLOR's project target, not a claim that every legitimate North American nameplate must use that exact notation. Ask the certifier to establish the acceptable supply range of the actual variant.
- Record Canada separately by intended province, exact variant and Canadian certification scope. A US mark or a generic North America claim does not establish the Canadian configuration.
- Select one ordinary production configuration before optional sensors, finishes, holders or accessory variants. Every variation needs a documented relationship to the approved construction; a private-label name or plug change is not presumed covered.
- This package is for document qualification and a later decision about controlled evaluation samples. It contains no hotel trial authorization. No candidate currently has a complete independently verified readiness dossier.
- Public records may contain source URLs, model identifiers, published specification claims and evidence status. Business licenses with personal details, quotations, BOMs, reports received in confidence, counterparties and legal conclusions belong only in the dedicated Aerlor restricted workspace after its permissions are verified.
