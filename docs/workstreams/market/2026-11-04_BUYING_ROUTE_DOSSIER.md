# AERLOR MARKET-01 — Buying routes and room-event qualification

**Evidence accessed: 2026-11-04. Status: public-source research, not a qualified sales pipeline.**

## Decision prepared

Prioritize a requirements-discovery batch through **Concord Hospitality, Benjamin West, Northland's Sutton Place Kelowna project, Guest Supply's category / New Hotel Opening Program, and DayBlink GPO**. This is an analyst-proposed sequence for learning about five different purchasing mechanisms. It is not a conversion ranking, an outreach authorization or a claim of demand.

The route to scale has to connect an exact-model guestroom requirement to a purchasing entity, a permitted alternate and an owner-approved room event. An attractive portfolio, opening announcement or supplier application does not complete that chain. Existing appliance brands already have hospitality and wholesale routes, so access and hotel-use support must be tested alongside product performance.

## How to read the evidence

**Documented** means the identified original source says the stated fact. Company service and product pages are self-descriptions, not independent audits. **Inferred** means our proposed routing or prioritization. **Unknown** is preserved where a source does not establish authority, funding, scope, timing, terms or willingness to evaluate.

All records are **NOT CONTACTED**. No AERLOR supplier acceptance, product approval, interview, funded dryer order, open specification window or purchase commitment was verified. An active or financed building project is recorded separately from a buyer-approved appliance budget. Owner/developer names refer to public organizations, not private individuals or contact CRM.

For each row, publication/event dates are distinguished from the access date. Undated pages are explicitly undated. Room counts are source-stated hotel units, with residences separated where available; no hotel/network totals are summed or converted into expected sales. The two Four Seasons projects share a brand gate and are not treated as independent evidence that the brand accepts an alternate. A purchasing agent, network, distributor and hotel may be alternate paths to the same eventual order.

**Priority:** A = first five discovery briefs; B = secondary queue; C = prerequisites or further evidence first. Rank is a research sequence, not a probability of conversion. Criteria are clarity of the purchasing mechanism, a traceable room event, learning value, and visible constraints. Missing private terms and buyer confirmation are not scored as positive evidence.

**Category evidence:** D = direct dryer-category listing; O = OS&E service only; P = general procurement / partner contracting; R = routing only; U = project known, category buyer not established. D does not mean AERLOR is eligible. Exact evidence and limitations are in the [machine-readable register](2026-11-04_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.json).
