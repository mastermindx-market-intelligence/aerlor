# AERLOR | Execution Charter
**Working document — 2026-11-04 | Pre-commercial | Public-safe**

> AERLOR is a **prelaunch design preview**, not an established manufacturer, approved vendor, or operating sales channel. See [README](../../README.md) and [LAUNCH.md](../../LAUNCH.md). This charter authorizes planning and validation; it does not appoint a legal corporate officer, commit funds, grant rights, or represent sales/procurement approvals.

## Mission and specific wedge

Help upscale and luxury hotels deliver an excellent, dependable getting-ready experience without excessive acquisition cost or operational complexity. Start with **one high-speed guestroom hair dryer**, supported by verified performance, exact-model safety/compliance, a commercial guestroom warranty, local spares and replacement processes. Test mirrors and borrowable styling tools through qualified partners **after** the core program demonstrates demand.

AERLOR should be judged as a product **and** a channel/service business. Attractive rendering, a manufacturer quote and catalog inclusion are not proof of demand.

## The strategy in one page

1. **Confirm procurement reality first.** Identify actual spending authority, brand constraints, opening/renovation projects and economically meaningful channel routes. A network's hotel count is *access*, not guaranteed orders.
2. **Find a product with defensible delivered economics.** Evaluate independently designed ODM platforms with exact-market safety, validation and FTO review; avoid product-clone assumptions.
3. **Build a measurable pilot offer.** Test against the incumbent plus at least one inexpensive high-speed alternative. Pre-agree prices and the date/criteria for a funded rollout decision.
4. **Choose the scalable channel using actual orders.** Prefer property operators with multi-property funding, appropriately structured distributors, amenity suppliers and project specifiers; don't confuse a GPO listing with a mandate.
5. **Expand only after earned evidence.** A differentiated finish, mirror program, lending kits, broader regions and consumer sales each require separate evidence and economics.

## Sequenced execution

### Gate A — Buyer and channel discovery (proposed first 2–3 weeks)
- Research routes and locate decision-makers at independent luxury networks, multi-property operators, project OS&E purchasing firms, and existing amenities distributors.
- Complete 15–25 structured buyer interviews with a mix of operations, purchasing and engineering stakeholders. These are **targets, not scheduled meetings**.
- Qualify at least three *specific* projects/owner groups with room counts, purchasing entity, decision timeline, requirement stack and contactable budget owner.
- Identify cases where an established franchise standard prohibits or complicates a new dryer.

### Gate B — Platform and compliance qualification (proposed weeks 2–6, overlapping discovery)
- RFQ at least three eligible OEM/ODM platforms and request physical evaluation samples.
- Confirm exact electrical SKU by market; obtain independent test-lab scope and import-broker classification/landed-cost estimate.
- Commission freedom-to-operate/trademark review by qualified counsel before tooling and production.
- Freeze a provisional hospitality specification: weight, controls, filters, thermal protection, cord/plug, care cycle, spares and warranty protocol.
- **No guestroom pilot with an unapproved/unverified product.**

### Gate C — Paid property tests (after technical readiness; proposed 6–8 week field period)
- Target three paid pilots, roughly 20–30 rooms each, with named owner/buyer and written decision date. Size and duration must be adapted to real hotel constraints.
- Measure performance, user preference across hair types, guest feedback, housekeeping labor, defects, loss, and replacement requests.
- Seek at least two independent rollout decisions with actual budget/PO evidence—not promises.

### Gate D — Scale and product expansion (after pilot evidence)
- Negotiate a bounded reseller/purchasing relationship with documented promotion, account ownership and economics.
- Reconcile contribution margins, warranty and payments/working capital before stock build.
- Only then consider differentiated finishes, mirrors, small styling-tool pool, export markets and retail channels.

## Decision rules / current state

- **Go to commercial pilots** only with identified buyer, appropriate verified product and meaningful paid scope.
- **Do not scale** on catalog acceptance or distributor introductions alone.
- **Do not claim** increased ADR, loyalty, superior hair protection, Dyson-equivalent performance, American manufacture, legally cleared patents or successful hotel partnership without evidence.
- **Do not confuse** an assumed consumer MSRP with established market value.
- Site release gates remain governed by [LAUNCH.md](../../LAUNCH.md); this charter does not override them.
