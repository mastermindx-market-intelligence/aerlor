# AERLOR | Avendra channel integration and implementation plan

**2026-11-05 | Public-safe proposal | Precommercial; no external commitments or approvals**

## Executive recommendation

**Treat Avendra as a gated distribution experiment, not AERLOR's initial route to market.** The first evidence of product-market fit should be a compliant product, a funded hotel buyer, a paid pilot and a real post-pilot purchase order. Avendra, Guest Supply and the AAHOA/Folio marketplace are potential multipliers after buyer activation and economics are demonstrated. A vendor listing without category eligibility, hotel approval, distributor activation and purchase orders is not traction.

Evidence companion: [2026-11-05 Avendra procurement audit](../research/2026-11-05_AVENDRA_PROCUREMENT_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md). The inaccessible expanded Deep Research report must be reconciled if later provided; this plan does not claim to have audited its full text.

## 2. New AVENDRA-01 evidence workstream

**Purpose:** determine whether Avendra/Guest Supply/AAHOA offers an economically viable, actually activatable path for AERLOR's first approved guestroom dryer SKU.

**Decision gate A — category:** named category owner; legal contracting party; guestroom electrical/room-operations category confirmed; live bid or documented off-cycle route; country/flag eligibility. The current official calendar, updated 2026-10-22 and rechecked 2026-11-06 at its March-named URL, lists C33327 "SPA - Salon Hair Care Products" with an estimated 2026-12-01 inquiry deadline. This is **not** evidence of an open guestroom hairdryer RFP; confirm category fit and the current cycle before relying on it.

**Decision gate B — economics:** exact hotel price or price tier from authorized sources; distributor sell-in and sellout distinguished; written GPO/program fee basis and timing; freight, tax, returns, warranty, stock, payment, chargebacks and price protection. The private finance owner must stress-test 100, 500, 2,000 and 10,000 units; unknown fees are not zero.

**Decision gate C — activation:** named purchasing organization with actual eligible properties, buyer authority, funded category budget, approved-alternate process and first-order mechanism. Network room count, directory listing and press releases are not demand.

**Decision gate D — product:** exact US/Canada electrical configuration and required independent verification, CPSC immersion protection, import/broker analysis, service stock, warranty, insurance, QA and traceability. Discussions can precede this; hotel deployment and stocking commitments cannot.

**Gate disposition:** PURSUE only when A–D pass and the private economics/working-capital gates are acceptable; DEFER if Avendra access is unclear but direct funded projects remain viable; DECLINE if fee stack, control, product eligibility or demand activation is structurally unacceptable.

## 4. Channel and buyer sequence

**P0 — direct evidence and funded orders:** owner/operator with actual property purchasing authority; new opening or renovation with OS&E budget; appointed project specifier. Ask who buys, which flag/engineer can veto, what existing dryer is installed, what exact budget is available and when the decision is made.

**P1 — multi-property activation:** independent luxury purchasing networks, hotel amenity suppliers and a limited distributor with named customers. Demand a credible first purchase/activation plan rather than only broad catalog placement.

**P2 — Avendra/Guest Supply/Folio option:** supplier category inquiry and fee diligence in parallel; no exclusivity, minimum stock or unconditional discount until real category eligibility and funded demand. Guest Supply inventory and Folio software are not themselves Avendra supplier awards.

**Fallback:** if a distributor margin or GPO fee stack defeats manufacturer contribution, keep direct project procurement and operator groups as primary routes. If the hotel buyer requires Dyson brand equity, treat that as a buyer preference to test rather than assert AERLOR is equivalent.

## 5. Buyer discovery and procurement RFI

For each target, record: property/flag, actual owner/operator, room count in the funded scope, current SKU and net delivered price, approval chain, planned purchase date, procurement route, exact requirements, responsible budget owner, pilot willingness, source URL and next action. **Do not store confidential account records or named contacts in this public repo.**

Minimum category RFI:
1. Which team owns guestroom hair dryers; which sourcing cycle applies?
2. What is the supplier approval sequence, and what does approval **not** provide?
3. Who is merchant of record in direct, stocked, drop-ship and Folio orders?
4. Are exact hotel SKU prices and prior-year category volumes available to an authorized buyer?
5. Which fee, rebate, marketing, chargeback and distributor terms apply, on which calculation bases?
6. What minimum inventory, lead time, local service and product insurance are required?
7. Who grants brand-standard alternate approval and pilot authorization?
8. Which named buyers have a funded opening/refresh and a decision date?
9. What are price-protection, returns, termination, sell-off, audit, exclusivity and MFN terms?
10. What concrete first-order activation will the channel provide after listing?

No outbound inquiries under this document without the existing Chairman authorization for truthful prelaunch representation.

## 6. Stage-gated product and buyer validation

**Stage 0 — product/bench:** compare exact candidate to current incumbent and one low-cost high-speed competitor; measure relevant performance, cleaning, heat/noise, attachments, electrical/cord behavior, QA and service requirements. No guestroom use before exact-market safety/legal clearance.

**Stage 1 — paid pilot:** propose three unrelated hotels with ~20–30 installed rooms each over 6–8 weeks, only after hotel/flag/engineer and product readiness. Require an actual budget owner, paid price, service terms, defined measurement protocol and a **pre-agreed post-pilot purchasing decision**.

**Stage 2 — controlled expansion:** consider 100–250 rooms across 2–5 properties only after Stage 1 provides real buyer orders/intent, acceptable product/service evidence and verified contribution. The 100–250-room idea is a **second-stage expansion**, not a replacement for the smaller initial validation design.

**Stage 3 — channel activation:** only after repeat paid orders, negotiate a stocked/marketplace route with activation obligations and contract protections. Do not confuse a signed supplier agreement with property-level deployment.

Pilot evidence: installed-room-days, guest preference with sample uncertainty, defects/returns, safety incidents, cleaning/engineering minutes, service lead time, actual purchase order and follow-on unit volume. No invented ADR lift, energy savings or brand prestige.

## 8. Risk register and contractual protections

| Failure mode | Observable trigger | Mitigation / stop |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commodity price anchor | Buyer only budgets replacement at <$30 | Seek funded premium amenity project, not ordinary replacement |
| GPO fee stack | Supplier net below private downside gate | Reprice, narrow scope, use direct route or decline |
| Passive listing | No named activation or first PO | No stocking or exclusivity |
| Brand/engineering veto | Exact product cannot be approved | Stop affected property; qualify alternate flag/project |
| Unsafe/unverified SKU | Missing exact-market evidence | No guestroom use, claims or production commitment |
| Cash strain | Long A/R plus supplier deposits/returns | Require deposits, PO-backed production and cash model |
| Stranded inventory | Broad returns, termination without sell-off | Cap stock; buyback/sell-off rights; staged release |
| Price-control loss | Unbounded MFN, retroactive rebates or price protection | Narrow definitions and duration; reject uncapped exposure |
| Source overclaim | Public price described as Avendra contract price | Correct immediately, preserve URL/date and qualification |

## 9. Progress scorecard and Chairman decision rights

**Evidence KPIs:** verified category owner and sourcing route; actual participating hotel buying authority; exact incumbent SKU/price records; qualified funded projects; written fee schedules; exact-SKU compliance status; authorized paid pilots; post-pilot POs; manufacturer contribution and working capital **in private finance**. Network membership and "supplier approved" are not sales KPIs.

**Chairman-reserved:** legal identity and public representation; outbound supplier/buyer contact; paid samples, lab, counsel, broker or tooling; contract/exclusivity; inventory and pilot commitments; public product/price claims; merger of strategic changes if company governance requires. This PR proposes research/plan integration only; it does not authorize those effects.

## 10. 12-month decision milestones

**Month 1:** evidence audit and integrated strategy; **Month 2:** category and buyer budget truth; **Month 3:** two independent paid pilot commitments or pivot; **Months 4–6:** compliant pilots, measured operations and actual rollout decision; **Months 7–9:** repeat paid orders and viable channel fee/cash terms; **Months 10–12:** measured portfolio/distributor expansion or explicit DEFER/DECLINE. Dates move if safety/approval takes longer; do not compress compliance.
