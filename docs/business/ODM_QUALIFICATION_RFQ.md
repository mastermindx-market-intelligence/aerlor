# AERLOR | High-Speed Hairdryer ODM Evaluation RFQ
**Draft for later authorized supplier engagement | 2026-11-04 | Not sent**

AERLOR is **prelaunch** and is reviewing whether a manufacturer can supply a legally marketable, independently designed guestroom high-speed hairdryer suitable for selected North American hotels. Nothing below is a purchase order, certification claim or price commitment.

Do not put live supplier pricing or supplier confidential BOM details into this public repository. Send RFQ individually through an approved business identity; preserve factory quotes in an access-controlled workspace.

## 1. What we are buying (qualification phase only)

The near-term deliverable is:
1. A verifiably existing independently designed **120 V / 60 Hz US pilot candidate** with documented applicable electrical compliance and hotel-use support;
2. A separate **Canadian-market** model/approval plan if Canada is the selected pilot market; and
3. A complete landed-cost and serviceability comparison at realistic small pilot and annual volume tiers.

Do not assume that a European 220–240 V listing is fit for US/Canadian outlets or that a China-only certificate covers the intended market. Do not describe the device as 'fanless'; expect an internal BLDC motor/impeller.

**Target product experience (subject to lab and user validation):**
- Comfortable handheld balance and hair-routine coverage including concentrator and appropriately offered diffuser.
- Controllable airflow and appropriate heat options; clear and accessible switches.
- Filter access for housekeeping, documented filter cleaning intervals, serviceable cord/plug/strain relief and durable attachments.
- Acceptable appearance under common hospitality cleaning protocols.
- Practical room storage/holder options without creating a hazard.
- Traceable labeling and replaceable/spare units.
- Verified reliability and thermal safety under real-use and blocked-filter cases.

Avoid prescribing rpm, wattage, noise or heat reduction as marketing claims before testing.

## 3. Required attachments before sample shortlist

1. Exact-model high-resolution photos, drawings and production bill of materials **under an NDA as needed** (do not release confidential BOM publicly).
2. Actual lab report numbers and traceable marking authorizations for the **same electrical configuration**.
3. Full instruction manual, maintenance/cleaning guide and warranty contract.
4. Prior production inspection/quality sample records and factory photo/video audit route.
5. Signed willingness to freeze BOM and notify/obtain approval before changes to motor, control board, cord, plug, thermal protection, housing, filter or supplier.
6. Authorized rights documentation for custom marks, finishes, any claims of exclusivity, and preexisting designs.

Reject supplier promises such as 'ETL certified' without documentation that can be independently validated with the relevant certifier.

## 4. Quote templates (financial terms to remain restricted)

Supplier should quote distinct scenarios:
- Samples: 3–5 units each across at least three independent candidate platforms.
- Pilot: 60–100 units total of chosen platform in one destination country.
- Scale: 500 / 1,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 units, same frozen BOM.
- Optional: unique finish and accessory packaging, but **not** an unapproved second motor/control architecture.

Total buyer-side cost = purchase terms + packaging/tooling/testing allocation + pre-shipment QA + international freight + customs duties/Chapter 99 if applicable + broker, warehousing, last mile + warranty/returns + quality loss. The selected source must satisfy **both** technical and landed-cost gates.

**Import duty caution:** Confirm HTS 8516.31 classification, origin and any additional measures with a licensed broker for the **anticipated entry date**. Rules may change and sources may disagree. https://hts.usitc.gov/

## 5. Technical testing protocol proposal (not a lab standard)

**Safety / reliability:** qualified lab determines actual required methods for electrical, overload, immersion, thermal cycling, abnormal filter obstruction, cord stress, drop/shock, cleaning agent interaction and labeling. Do not treat a founder-run test as regulatory compliance.

**Independent product comparison:** benchmark at least one common Conair hospitality dryer, Laifen (appropriate region), Dyson or another genuinely premium benchmark, and each shortlisted ODM candidate. Use fixed ambient conditions, consistent hair samples/hair routines and blinded participants when feasible. Measure drying time, temperature distribution, perceived comfort, noise by calibrated methodology, power, weight/ergonomics, filter-cleaning time and attachment loss.

**Qualitative buyer test:** show product **without brand** first; then branded and priced choices. Do not claim North-American name preference without this test.

**Extended field test:** short pilots can validate staff workflow and early failures; they do **not** establish 4–10-year durability. Model the confidence limit transparently.

## 6. Supplier exclusion criteria (hard gates)

- Refuses to identify real legal manufacturer or manufacturing location.
- Relies on generic safety marks but cannot supply verifiable exact-model documents.
- Design copies a recognizable protected ornamental form or makes implausible blanket patent-expiry claims.
- Product cannot comply with the destination market's relevant electrical safety requirements.
- No written commercial hotel-use warranty and no credible spare/replacement plan.
- Uncontrolled BOM substitutions, unsafe cord/thermal construction or unverifiable traceability.
- Quotations hide shipping, legal import exposure, changes to packaging, or minimum orders in a way that defeats margin/cash planning.

## 7. Pilot readiness decision

A manufacturer **may supply lab evaluation samples** after a limited, approved sample order and lawful import. **Do not install products in hotel guestrooms** until competent product safety and legal review, appropriate insurance, hotel engineering acceptance and written pilot terms are complete.

Before any tooling or production investment, require:
- Product FTO/design and brand/trademark clearance from qualified counsel;
- Exact market compliance approval as required;
- Customs broker landed-cost estimate, including live duty/tariff treatment;
- Evidence of real buyer demand and a financially viable distribution structure;
- Written supplier and after-sales agreements.
