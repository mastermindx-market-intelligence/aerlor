## 60-second orientation
AERLOR is a **precommercial premium guestroom haircare / getting-ready venture**. The goal is to develop and supply reliable high-speed hairdryers and eventually selected beauty/wardrobe devices for upscale and luxury hotels. The value thesis is better hotel guest experience at scalable purchasing cost, backed by commercial warranty and local support.

The **business constraint** is procurement distribution, not only manufacturing. Hotels can afford a $69–99 hypothetical dryer but may not approve replacement of a working incumbent. Pursue real budget authorities at operators, funded new openings/renovations, category buyers, OS&E specifiers, amenities partners and GPOs; do not count a network listing as an order. A consumer-facing premium MSRP is a brand hypothesis, not validated prestige.

## Founder's stated direction (not yet an approved commercial offer)
- Premium US-positioned-sounding brand, China/Shenzhen ODM manufacturing, globally distributable hotel hairdryer line.
- Potential Essential (lightweight) and Signature/Pro (more premium visual finish), with volume-price concepts around $69–99 and real retail-priced positioning; **all pricing hypothetical**.
- Proposed later category expansion into mirrors, curlers/straighteners, styling kits, steamers, toothbrush kits and slippers. Prioritize observed guest routines, safe/hygienic loaner handling and verified buyer economics, not gender stereotypes.
- Founder has ecommerce/manufacturing and import/export experience, but no assumed hotel procurement access.

## Key competitive facts to challenge
- International hotel dryer and premium beauty device distribution already exists: Dyson, Laifen bulk, Conair/BaBylissPRO, JVD, Aliseo, Vanity Group WAVES, CHI/Guest Supply, T3/SLX and others.
- Some hotels deliberately deploy Dyson or styling tool loaner programs.
- Brand prestige of a *new* North American name vs Laifen remains **hypothesis**.
- Avoid unqualified claim that classic hotel dryers regularly fail; intermittent use can support long service.

## Non-negotiable gates
- Supplier/product claims must be corroborated by exact SKU test reports and, when required, qualified independent engineering/legal review.
- US/Canada electricity and importer customs treatment are jurisdiction-specific. No automatic patent expiry or design-clone clearance.
- No external outreach, purchases, contracts, trademark applications or real guestroom trials without the required Chairman authorization, responsible company identity and compliance.
- No confidential financial sheets, partner emails, deal terms or buyer identities in **public** GitHub. Drive/Notion permissions must be checked.
- No fake hotel logos, MSRP reference prices, performance numbers, certification, supply availability or actual partner claims on website.

## Definition of progress
**Useful progress** = an independently checkable decision or artifact: actual source + date, buyer role identified, verified spec, supplier evidence request, validated modeling assumptions, runnable code/test/preview, actual purchase interest. A formatted report with no new evidence is not by itself launch progress.

**Highest-value next company milestone:** an actual qualified purchaser's specification + a plausible compliant ODM platform + funded pilot path with acceptable contribution economics.
