## Hard requirements
US: CPSC integral immersion protection, correct 120V/60Hz product. Canada: Canadian-recognized approvals and local electrical rules. Importer/broker: item classification, current Chapter 99 duties, total tariff and freight. Any brand/design FTO remains legal-counsel territory. Do not ship a supplier sample into a hotel guestroom without competence and signed terms.

## Acceptance
- Three exact SKU candidates **or** a documented failure showing no candidate currently evidences necessary approval.

- Explicit rank: verified, supplier-claimed, unverified, disqualified; no invented warranty/performance results.

## Stop conditions
