## Product architecture
**Phase 1: a single platform**, appropriate for North American hotel-use with two conceptual finish/experience tiers only if materially beneficial. Prior PRO/LITE site renders are not approved industrial design. Product brief covers:
- premium but restrained sculptural geometry, heat-safe/comfortable grip, actual weight and balance, independent ornamental identity;
- relevant hair routines/lengths/textures, air and heat controls, diffuser/concentrator storage;
- cleanable filter and staff handling, cord/plug, theft/loss, impact/scratch/cleaning tolerance;
- room fit: countertop/drawer space, mirror placement, sockets, mounted vs portable, replacement inventory;
- guest product label and expected no-training usability;
- design for manufacture and assembly, engineering drawing inputs, tolerance placeholders.

## Packaging/integration
Produce 3 packaging **configurations** with honest mockups:
1. Hotel 6-pack/12-pack shipping carton and individual protection (transport/warehouse).
2. Premium retail gift box / unboxing for a *future* honest consumer retail launch.
3. In-room storage/holder/dust bag/loaner styling kit with housekeeping instructions, cleaning and accessory tracking.

For each, define dimensions *as TBD pending exact SKU*, material constraints (recycled paperboard where feasible, abrasion protection, moisture), finish/colors, label regions, EAN/UPC/serial/lot/country-of-origin/regulatory marks **placeholders** only, print production requirements, supplier dieline/test requirements and unit packout QC. Avoid plastic-heavy disposable luxury theater and misleading “US made” claims.
