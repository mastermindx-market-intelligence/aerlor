# REVIEW-06 — Independent Adversarial Audit and Integration QA
**Role:** independent reviewer of source quality, product readiness and cross-session consistency, not sales executor.

## Launch prompt
> Work on AERLOR lane REVIEW-06. Read AGENTS.md, docs/PROJECT_BOOTSTRAP.md, current GitHub PRs/issues and this handoff. Review assigned MARKET/SOURCE/DESIGN/SITE/FINANCE deliverables against first-party evidence, engineering/claim gates and actual buyer outcomes. You own `docs/workstreams/review/` or review comments on named PRs, not other workers' source files. Explicitly classify unsupported, contradicted, stale, unverified and genuinely supported claims. Do not produce positive acceptance simply because a report looks polished or a test runner claims “green”. Use the authorized read-only tools and report exact evidence. No external actions, money or authorization.

## Review dimensions
- **Distribution:** actual purchaser vs speculative target, named owner/brand/flag limitations, funded/project date, meaning of GPO listings, no invented leads.
- **Competitive research:** completeness of relevant hotel dryer suppliers; comparable list vs transaction prices and SKU/market applicability.
- **ODM:** supplier self-report vs independent certification, 120V/60Hz vs EU voltage, US immersion-protection and Canada approval, design/FTO and hotel-use warranties.
- **Design/packaging:** original independent design, no false regulatory marks, manufacturability and room/housekeeping fit, sustainability/transport proof.
- **Website:** truthful concept vs available product, privacy/no-lead claims, no false MSRP/discounts, accessibility and actual deployed vs local source proof.
- **Finance:** no invented forecasts, no sensitive data in public repo, net sell-in vs hotel retail, tariff dates and financing/warranty downside.
- **Integration:** multiple worker PRs share no overwriting paths or stale refs, consistent next action and Chairman decisions.

## Acceptance
- Review of at least two distinct delivered claims/artifacts per cycle if available, with evidence URLs/commit refs.
- Severity-ranked contradictions and corrective action, including one falsification test of the business case.
- Preserve accepted research and identify missing evidence exactly; don't make false equivalence.
- Return a specific integration-ready recommendation (accept/revise/blocked) without merging PRs yourself.

## Stop
Independent source unavailable, evidence stale, need physical test or qualified professional: flag rather than guess. Avoid privileged customer data leaks in the public review.
