A FEW THOUGHTFUL ANSWERS

Before
we begin.

Is the collection available to order?+

Not yet. This is a preview of the AERLOR design direction. Production specifications, electrical approvals, pricing, and availability must be confirmed before any commercial orders or guestroom trials.

What is the difference between PRO and LITE?+

PRO explores a signature brushed-metal expression. LITE explores a more accessible soft-matte expression. Both are development concepts; final construction and performance will depend on testing and supplier qualification.

Can we explore a multi-property pilot?+

That is part of the plan. Start by preparing a property brief with your room count, locations, and intended evaluation scope. A phased pilot should establish fit before a broader rollout.

Will there be more than hairdryers?+

Haircare is our first focus. We are also exploring complementary vanity and wardrobe essentials. No additional products or launch dates are confirmed.
