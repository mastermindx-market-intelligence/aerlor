A FEW THOUGHTFUL ANSWERS

Before
we begin.

Is the collection available to order?+

Not yet. This is a preview of the AERLOR design direction. Production specifications, electrical approvals, pricing, and availability must be confirmed before any commercial orders or guestroom trials.

What is the difference between Premium and Lite?+

Premium explores a signature brushed-metal expression. LITE explores a more accessible soft-matte expression. Both are development concepts; final construction and performance will depend on testing and supplier qualification.

Can we explore a multi-property pilot?+

That is part of the plan. Start by preparing a property brief with your room count, locations, and intended evaluation scope. A phased pilot should establish fit before a broader rollout.

Will there be more than hairdryers?+

Explore the complete development collection: hairdryers, illuminated makeup mirrors and curling tools, each in Lite and Premium expressions. Product pages explain the design direction, intended placement and the technical details required before release.
