PORTFOLIO PLANNING

Define the common standard.
Document the local differences.

One visual language does not mean one configuration everywhere. A portfolio program needs a property matrix: destination, room type, product, quantity and delivery phase.

Keep the approved model and market evidence attached to each configuration. Then use a limited pilot to test the operational fit.

What a portfolio brief should include
Planning areaRecord
Property & room mixLocations, brands, room categories and equipped room counts.
Product standardLite or Premium, finish, model revision and required accessories.
DestinationVoltage, plug, applicable evidence and engineering acceptance.
OperationsCare instructions, lending procedure where relevant, spares and service.
Commercial planComplete quotation, purchasing authority and phased delivery.
