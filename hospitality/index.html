THE GUEST EXPERIENCE

Make the everyday feel like an upgrade.

A room is used, not just photographed. Focus on the moments guests repeat every day.

01

A familiar routine.

A useful dryer, a well-positioned mirror and access to a styling tool. Less improvising before breakfast, a meeting or a night out.

02

A coherent room.

Coordinate the equipment with the interior. Match Lite or Premium to your room categories instead of adding unrelated appliances.

03

A thoughtful service.

Put the essentials in the room. Offer selected styling tools on request where a managed lending service makes more sense.
