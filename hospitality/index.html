START WITH EVIDENCE, NOT A PROPERTY-WIDE LEAP

Make the first rooms
your best argument.

Choose a limited pilot after the product and market approvals are ready. Give operations a baseline and guests a voice. Scale what genuinely works.

  1. Define the room standard

    Room types, guest routines, placement, voltage and the service you want to provide.

  2. Evaluate the actual product

    Engineering, housekeeping and a defined sample review—not a decision made from a rendering.

  3. Measure the experience

    Guest feedback, amenity requests, service incidents, cleaning time and staff observations.

  4. Build a funded rollout

    Confirm the commercial terms, spare pool, delivery phases and responsibilities.
