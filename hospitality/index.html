A BETTER ROOM STANDARD

Start with three questions.

A product needs to work for the guest and for the people responsible for the room.

01

Does it suit your guests?

Consider everyday routines, room category and whether an amenity belongs in every room or is better offered on request.

02

Does it work for your team?

Review handling, location, cleaning, storage and the process for removing a faulty unit from service.

03

Is the purchase fully specified?

Keep the exact model, destination approvals, complete price, delivery plan and support terms in the same record.
