DESIGN STUDY / AERLOR

A coherent material story.
A practical room plan.

Explore soft-matte and metal-finish directions across a complete getting-ready collection. Then test how the actual product sits in the room: reach, light, outlets, cable routing and cleaning clearance.

Concept artwork is a conversation starter, not a fabrication drawing. Final dimensions, materials and installation requirements must come from the approved model.

Coordinate

Choose a finish direction, a room category and the visual relationship between the appliances and the interior.

Validate

Review an actual sample alongside the room mock-up. Include engineering and housekeeping in the decision.

Document

Specify the approved model and revision. Keep technical approvals, care requirements and commercial terms together.

Technical asset policy

We will not present concept dimensions or generated CAD as approved production drawings. Use the product specification briefs to record what must be confirmed.
