BUY THE ROOM STANDARD, NOT JUST THE UNIT

A more complete
procurement conversation.

Choosing a guestroom appliance is not only a design decision. It is a question of electrical compatibility, daily handling, replacement needs and who stands behind the exact model you receive.

Build the brief once. Use it to align your owner, purchasing team, engineering and housekeeping before agreeing the final commercial offer.

01 / EVALUATE

Sample & specification

Define the intended configuration and required evidence. Sample availability and terms must be confirmed before payment.

02 / VALIDATE

Property pilot

Use a defined room group, a clear scorecard and a controlled service plan to test fit.

03 / STANDARDISE

Full-property rollout

Plan quantities, spares, destination requirements and phased delivery around the property.

04 / SCALE

Portfolio program

Create a common specification, then account for each location’s electrical and operational needs.

WHAT A COMPLETE QUOTE SHOULD TELL YOU

No important detail
left to inference.

The product

Exact model and revision, finish, electrical version, accessories, unit quantities and approved specification.

The commercial offer

Currency, unit and volume pricing, taxes, freight, duties where relevant, payment schedule and quote validity.

The delivery & support

Agreed lead time, delivery destinations, warranty scope, spare parts and the process for service or replacement.
