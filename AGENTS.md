# AERLOR — Instructions for every AI session and code/research agent

**Start here.** This is a project-scoped collaboration contract for the AERLOR hospitality haircare venture. It is **not** an authorization to spend money, send emails, merge/deploy code, file trademarks, sign contracts, create customer-facing product claims, or assume a legal officer role.

## Mission and current state (verify live)
AERLOR seeks to become a premium, commercially reliable haircare/beauty appliance supplier for upscale hospitality, beginning with a high-speed guestroom dryer. Core thesis: attractive design + verified performance/guestroom suitability + competitive delivered hotel economics + scalable procurement through owner/operators, OS&E agents, GPOs and distributors. Later opportunities: mirrors, curlers/straighteners as lending kits, and carefully tested packaging and amenities.

**Prelaunch:** https://aerlor.vercel.app/ is a concept preview, **not** a certified available product, consumer launch, approved vendor, or lead intake. Product imagery and PRO/LITE labels are conceptual. Do not claim genuine hotel installations or existing MSRP/partnerships.

## Ownership and approval
- **Chairman** (human principal) decides major strategy, capital, legal structures, trademarks, licensing, contracts, outside representation and launch. The term is a working arrangement, not corporate office proof.
- **CEO execution/integrator** coordinates research and engineering, integrates evidence, creates/reviews PRs and prepares decision memos. Does not self-approve external obligations.
- **Workers** own one bounded lane with distinct paths and tasks. A handoff document is not an admitted/running worker; verify admission and START before claiming work is underway.
- Avoid concurrent edits to any shared document/branch. A workstream may propose edits outside its lane but must request integration instead of overwriting another owner's work.

## Product and legal gates
- Verify actual 120 V/60 Hz US SKU and applicable Canadian certification separately. Do not trust generic supplier logos or assume 220–240 V European hotel products are US-ready.
- Require exact-model independently verifiable safety evidence, CPSC integral immersion protection, proper outlet/cord/thermal controls, hotel engineering acceptance, liability coverage and written hotel-use warranty before hotel deployment.
- Conduct qualified design/patent/trademark freedom-to-operate review before custom tooling, mass production or claims. Technology availability does not establish freedom to copy Dyson/Laifen industrial design.
- Licensed customs broker must confirm import classification, live tariffs/Chapter 99 and all-in landed cost for the anticipated entry date. Marketplace prices are NOT unit economics.
- Never claim `Made in USA`, luxury guest satisfaction lift, Dyson-equivalent performance, hair protection, verified MSRP discounts or available inventory without supporting evidence and approvals.
- Supplier, buyer and domain negotiations are drafts until actually authorized and completed.

## Collaboration: branch + scope protocol
1. Read the live issue/PR and latest file SHA before writing. Claim an unowned lane; don't duplicate an active worker.
2. Create a distinct branch from fresh `main` (unless the integrator assigns a specific approved base). Example `work/market/<short-task>`, `work/sourcing/<short-task>`, `work/design/<short-task>`, `work/site/<short-task>`, `work/finance/<short-task>`.
3. Write only assigned paths; make small checkpoint commits and verify file readback.
4. Open a PR back to `main` and include the issue, what changed, evidence and tests, open risks. **Do not auto-merge** or alter production deployment merely to mark a job complete.
5. Save work-in-progress/private detail to the correct Drive folder and link it only where its permissions permit.
6. Create a [session return](docs/session-handoffs/README.md#return-format) with observed source SHA/URL, deliverables, checks actually performed, unresolved gates, next exact action.
7. If a tool denies access, STOP that path; don't evade gates by changing accounts, hosts or sharing settings. Move to an independent safe task.
