05 / BEFORE YOU SPECIFY

Useful answers,
before the first order.

Clear questions are better than premature promises. Here is what a buyer should establish at this stage.

Are AERLOR products available for wholesale orders?

The collection is in development. A commercial order requires approved exact models, jurisdiction-specific documentation, a seller, pricing, stock and agreed supply terms.

Can we review samples or request a custom finish?

Sample access, finishes, customization and minimum order quantities depend on the finalized model and supplier offer. They are not represented as available at this stage.

Why create a brief before asking for a price?

The meaningful price depends on intended model, quantity, market, site conditions and service requirements. The brief records those factors without pretending a quotation has been issued.

Does the planner submit my details?

No. The current planner is a private tool that downloads a project brief directly to your device. It does not transmit the information to AERLOR.
