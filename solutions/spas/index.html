06 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Clarity before
commitment.

Product development is not the same as a released commercial offering. These answers define what needs to be verified.

Can the dryer be installed near showers or wet changing areas?

Do not assume wet-area suitability. Electrical and placement restrictions depend on the exact certified model, jurisdiction and site conditions, and must be cleared by the relevant engineer.

Can we purchase or receive an evaluation unit today?

Availability, sample terms, shipping and pricing have not been established for a released commercial model. Use the brief to define what you would need to evaluate.

How should an initial rollout be planned?

Document actual use, site approvals, quantities and a sample acceptance protocol. Commercial procurement and any larger deployment follow only after evidence and written terms.
