## Research quality and exclusions

- All twelve included source entries were directly opened; relevant sections were inspected. Two research helpers supplied findings; the integrating session independently reopened the cited primary URLs and checked key passages.
- General manufacturer marketing is evidence of the manufacturer's published offer/instructions only. It is not comparative lab evidence.
- Large Dyson technical-specification and FEFCO PDFs exceeded the integrating web reader's size limit. Their detailed technical/version claims were **not used** as verified facts in this packet.
- Search indexing dates were not substituted for document revision dates. The Conair printed model identifiers and copyright were checked instead of inferred from its filename.
- The public preview initially failed in the search reader; later direct HTTP readback succeeded as recorded above. This was not an account/permission workaround.
- Source URLs can change. Before physical engineering or print release, recheck governing instructions, applicable standards/current editions, exact SKU and commercial terms. No external party was contacted by this research.
