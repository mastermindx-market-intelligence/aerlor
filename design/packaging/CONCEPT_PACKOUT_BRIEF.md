# AERLOR — Guestroom Appliance and Packaging System
**Concept requirements | 2026-11-04 | NOT final engineering, safety labels, dielines, packaging purchase or production photography.**

## Design objective

Create a premium **hospitality-ready getting-ready experience** for diverse hair and beauty routines—without the waste, bulk, staff burden or inflated cost of unnecessary consumer packaging in every room. AERLOR has no certified electrical SKU yet. Geometry, weight, wattage, cord, attachment interface, dimensions, volumes, factory tooling and landed cost are **TBD**.

## Product families, staged

| Concept | Phase | Function | Required proof before implementation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **AERLOR Essential** | Proposed initial | Balanced, lightweight high-speed hairdryer; useful heat/air controls; easy filter access; one or two genuine styling attachments | Noninfringing original design, exact 120 V/60 Hz and local approvals, verified thermal/electrical tests, hotel-use warranty |
| **AERLOR Signature** | Optional differentiation | Same verified core platform where practical, distinctive surface treatment, purposeful styling accessories or finish | Real guest/buyer value; housing, thermal, drop/scratch and certification impact; weight does not worsen ergonomics |
| **AERLOR Style Kit** | Later lending pilot | Curling or straightening tools that can be pre-reserved, inspected and sanitized under hotel procedure | Separate electrical approval, staff handling, burn/storage risks, checkout/loss accounting and buyer demand |
| **AERLOR Light** | Partner-sourced pilot | Usable illuminated vanity/makeup mirror | Electrical/optical safety, power draw, stable housing, hotel cleaning, outlet/surface suitability |

Keep the first electrical BOM a single platform until customer and manufacturer evidence supports variation. “Brushless” is not “fanless”: the motor drives an internal impeller.

## Visual language: original, not a Dyson/Laifen clone

- Forms: a distinctive restrained handle-body junction, readable controls and a visual hierarchy that looks at home in premium hotel bathrooms; do **not** copy protected circular barrel/hollow head configurations.
- Finish exploration: warm pearl, soft graphite, brushed champagne **as visual concepts**, with material actualities to be chosen after thermal/drop testing.
- Hospitality presentation: quiet confidence, legible typography, no huge consumer ad copy, no conspicuous claims of American manufacturing.
- Keep branding legible at arm’s length without demanding that a guest recognize an unknown name as existing luxury.
- Design for users with long, short, straight, wavy, curly and coily hair; analyze grip, drying routine and appropriate diffuser/concentrator needs.

## Three packaging modes

### 1. HOTEL BULK / OS&E DELIVERY
**Goal:** low damage, easy receiving, traceability and minimal waste, not a gift-box in every room.
- Proposed **6-unit and 12-unit master cartons** to price/test. Actual carton geometry after final product dimensions and protective packaging evaluation.
- Each unit in abrasion-safe, recyclable paperboard/pulp where proven, protective sleeve and necessary regulatory/user manual (language/territory dependent).
- Outside: SKU, voltage/country, batch/serial ranges, count, carton weight, handling and receiving reference. **No invented safety listing logos.**
- Test ISO/ISTA-relevant distribution vibration, drop, humidity and stacking with packaging engineer; record damage rates from pilots.

### 2. CONSUMER / FUTURE RETAIL
**Goal:** genuine premium retail perception if/when a legitimate priced product launches.
- Rigid or folding premium pack concept, protective insert, minimalist product presentation and a verified, original brand narrative.
- Accessory positions based on actual supplier packout and verified SKU; no stock CGI passed as physical product.
- Product photo/CAD only after final dimensions and rights. Print inserts and warranty copy match actual legal seller and coverage.
- Do not imply a fake retail compare-at price or permanent $329 discount. All consumer MSRP positions must be bona fide and substantiated.

### 3. IN-ROOM STORAGE / STYLING LENDING KIT
**Goal:** an appliance guests can locate/operate without instructions and housekeeping can maintain.
- Proposed slip-resistant countertop holder, bag or drawer tray **only if demonstrated as safe** for warm tools and room electrical layout.
- Attachment inventory card/QR linking to truthful care instructions (no data capture unless approved).
- Styling loaner pool: durable soft/hard case as appropriate, heat-resistant safe-cooling handling, staff sign-out/check-in, cleaning, serial number and loss/damage policy.
- Packaging should respect guests' ability to request accessories without special disclosure about their appearance, gender or routines.

## Required dieline / label zones (TBD by SKU)
- **Regulatory:** real listed electrical rating, correct product model, manufacturer/importer, country of origin, warning/instruction text and only genuine marks supported by certifier approval.
- **Ops:** serial/batch, case counts, instructions and hotel procurement SKU.
- **Brand:** wordmark, verified performance claims, languages and licensed trademarks if actually granted.
- **Logistics:** carton dimensions/weight, pallet limits, freight class and recycling material codes where valid.

## Design → engineering → procurement test sequence

1. Buyer requirements from MARKET-01: portable/mounted preference, current model and storage, staff care routine, price tier, loss/theft.
2. Design mockups and room-fit schematics from DESIGN-03 (concept).
3. Exact physical ODM samples and verified component dimensions from SOURCE-02.
4. User testing with diverse hair routines, objective performance/temperature/handling protocols from competent product engineering.
5. Packaging prototypes + drop/vibration + cleanability/storage trials.
6. Manufacturer approved mold/dieline/print test; independent IP and safety review; honest claims/country-of-origin.
7. Pilot shipment through legal/importer route and hotel engineering signoff.
8. After paid pilot, freeze SKU, packaging and actual replenishment/after-sales program.

## Questions for the next design workstream
- Does a differentiated finish justify added production and cleaning cost? Who within hotels values it?
- What exact product geometry can be original, ergonomically comfortable and approved under the intended electrical requirements?
- How do we make the filter visible and easy to clean while resisting lint and bathroom humidity?
- What can safely sit inside a guest drawer or a reception lending kit after operation?
- How few pieces/accessories achieve a genuinely better guest result?
- Can factory/packaging sources meet a durable carton and straightforward receiving process at viable per-unit landed cost?

**No physical product, production packaging, drawings or image renders have been produced by this brief.** This is a design input and cross-team contract, not a product acceptance or manufacturing order.
