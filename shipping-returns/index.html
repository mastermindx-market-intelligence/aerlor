Delivery, returns & warranty

Updated 4 November 2026

Before purchase

The collection is in development. No stock or delivery date is promised. A released product must have an agreed commercial offer before payment is accepted.

Delivery

The offer should state the destination, currency, dispatch or delivery window, freight, taxes, relevant duties and responsibility for delivery arrangements.

Returns and cancellations

Applicable terms must be presented before purchase. Retail, bespoke and wholesale orders may have different terms. These notes do not exclude any mandatory legal rights.

Hospitality-use warranty

Confirm that the exact product and intended hotel use are covered in writing. The offer should identify the warranty provider, term, coverage and fault-reporting process.

Questions

Include your order or quote reference. Do not include card numbers or payment credentials.
