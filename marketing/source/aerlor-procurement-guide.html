AERLORUnderstand the complete cost

A QUOTE YOU CAN ACTUALLY COMPARE

Look beyond
the unit price.

Exact product, finish, electrical configuration and included accessories
Unit quantities and any spare pool, stated separately
Currency, unit prices and the basis for any volume pricing
Taxes, freight, duties and destination responsibilities where applicable
Payment schedule, quotation validity and order-acceptance terms
Agreed production and delivery timing, with dependencies stated
Warranty scope, service process and replacement arrangements
Any customisation, packaging, artwork or sample charges
Compare like with like

A low unit price is not comparable to a complete landed and supported offer unless the included costs and obligations match. Do not infer an approval, accessory, warranty or delivery date from a generic catalogue description.

For multiple destinations, separate the electrical configurations and delivery responsibilities. A common visual design does not establish one universal international approval.

AERLORFrom selection to order

A CLEAR COMMERCIAL HANDOFF

Know what each
step means.

01

Project bag

A planning selection of products, finishes and quantities. It is not stock allocation or an order.

02

Project inquiry

A request to discuss requirements. An email draft is not sent until you send it. Direct submission must return a real delivery reference.

03

Written quotation

The product configuration, scope, pricing, delivery and applicable commercial terms should be explicit.

04

Approved checkout

Only released, priced and eligible configurations can proceed to payment. Card details belong on the payment provider’s hosted checkout.

05

Payment and fulfillment

Payment must be verified with the provider. A success-page visit is not proof of payment, and payment confirmation is not shipping confirmation.

Current collection status

AERLOR is in development. Pricing, market approval, availability and service commitments require confirmation before a commercial order can be accepted.
