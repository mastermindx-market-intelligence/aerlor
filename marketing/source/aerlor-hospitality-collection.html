AERLORThe room ritual

THE ROOM, THROUGH YOUR GUEST’S EYES

Three moments.
One considered experience.

A morning routine. The final look in the mirror. The preparations for a memorable evening. These moments are personal, even when the room is not their own.

Dry.

Give the everyday haircare ritual a deliberate place in the room standard.

Reflect.

Think about light, reach and the practicality of getting ready together.

Step out.

Offer a thoughtfully managed styling service for the moments that call for it.

The opportunity is not simply to put more appliances in more rooms. It is to remove a compromise at the point where your guest is preparing to meet the day.

More consideration. Not more clutter.

AERLORLite & Premium

TWO EXPRESSIONS / ONE PHILOSOPHY

Your room mix.
Your point of view.

Use the collection as a planning system. Lite explores a soft-matte everyday expression; Premium explores a more distinctive metal-finish presence. Neither tier implies a verified performance advantage.

Room / serviceConsidered approachDecision to validate
Everyday guestroomLite dryer and mirror directionPlacement, usability, care and value
Premium room / suitePremium dryer and mirror directionFinish fit, guest preference and support
On-request stylingA shared Lite or Premium curler poolDemand, safety, storage and lending routine
Multiple propertiesA common brief with local configurationsMarket approvals, voltage, plug and logistics

For the guest

A coherent experience, communicated clearly: what is in the room and what can be requested.

For the property

A specification that includes handling, cleaning, spares and service—not just appearance.

Make room for different routines

Hair texture, styling habits, eyesight, mobility and trip purpose vary. Evaluate the needs of your own guests rather than assuming one setup is right for everyone.

AERLORFrom vision to rooms

A THOUGHTFUL PATH TO ROLLOUT

Make the first rooms
your best argument.

01

Frame the experience

Map the room categories, guest routines, proposed placement and destination requirements.

02

Approve the actual model

Review a sample with engineering and housekeeping. Attach technical evidence to the exact configuration.

03

Run a defined pilot

Measure the guest response alongside service calls, cleaning time and operating issues.

04

Agree the complete offer

Confirm product revision, quantities, pricing, tax, freight, lead time, warranty and service responsibilities.

05

Roll out with a record

Keep the approved specification, care instructions, spare pool and feedback loop together.

Do not skip the release gates

Guestroom trials and commercial orders depend on qualified products and market approvals. A concept rendering is not permission to place an unapproved electrical product into service.
