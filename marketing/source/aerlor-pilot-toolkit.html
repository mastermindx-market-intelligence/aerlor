AERLORPre-launch checklist

BEFORE A GUEST TOUCHES THE PRODUCT

Safety first.
Operations alongside it.

Exact production or trial model is identified and matches its supporting documentation.
Engineering has accepted the market, voltage, plug and installation/location suitability.
Any temperature, shutoff or heat-rest feature used in the safety plan has been verified.
Clear guest instructions are available in the appropriate language and format.
Housekeeping has the approved cleaning, cooling, storage and inspection instructions.
The placement avoids unsafe routing, unstable surfaces or unsuitable wet-area use.
A fault-reporting, removal-from-service and replacement process is in place.
For lending tools: checkout, return, cooling and inspection responsibilities are assigned.
Staff know the pilot’s purpose, dates and feedback collection process.
The property has approved the pilot under its own risk and procurement procedures.
Engineering approval
Housekeeping approval
Operations approval
Stop conditions

Remove a unit from use if a safety concern arises. Guest satisfaction is not a substitute for safety approval. Escalate through the property’s incident process.
