AERLOR INFORMATION

Privacy

Updated 4 November 2026

Browsing

Browsing does not create an account or subscribe you to marketing. No advertising pixels or behavioural analytics are installed.

Your project bag

The project bag stores product identifiers, finishes and quantities on your device. It does not store your name, email, inquiry text or payment details. Clear the bag at any time. When storage is blocked, the bag lasts for the current page session only.

Project inquiries

Form details stay in the page until you choose a delivery action. An email draft is reviewed and sent in your email application. Preparing or downloading a brief does not transmit it. If direct submission is enabled, the form identifies the send action and reports a delivery reference only after the configured service accepts the inquiry.

Checkout

Payment remains disabled until commercial requirements are met. When enabled, card and billing information is entered on Stripe’s hosted checkout. AERLOR does not collect card numbers in a site form. The order-status service returns limited status information, not a public customer record.

Technical services

Hosting providers process requests to serve the site. Google Fonts receives requests to display the typeface. An enabled abuse-verification service is disclosed before submission. Transaction records remain in configured server-side services.

Contact

Use the business contact for privacy questions. Before commercial orders open, the responsible seller, operational retention policy and applicable terms must be published and approved.
