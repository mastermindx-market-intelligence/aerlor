AERLOR / PRIVACY

Privacy

Website information · updated 4 November 2026

The current experience

This site presents a collection in development. Browsing does not create an account or subscribe you to marketing. No advertising pixels or behavioural analytics are installed.

Your project bag

The project bag uses local storage on your device for product identifiers, finishes and quantities only. It does not store your name, email, payment details or inquiry text. You can clear the bag at any time. Blocking storage does not prevent browsing; the bag then lasts only for the current page session.

Project inquiries

Personal details entered into a project form stay in the page until you choose a delivery action. When the form offers an email draft, you review and send it in your own email application. Preparing a draft or downloading a brief does not transmit it. If direct submission is enabled, the form states that it will send the inquiry and returns a delivery reference only after the configured service accepts it.

Checkout

Online payment is disabled until the commercial launch requirements are met. When enabled, card and billing information is entered on Stripe’s hosted checkout. AERLOR does not ask you to type card numbers into this site. The order-status service returns a limited payment status, not an exposed customer record.

Technical services

Hosting providers process technical requests to serve the website. Google Fonts receives requests to display the typefaces. When enabled, a verification service may check form abuse; the site discloses that before submission. Essential transaction and request records are handled only through configured server-side services.

Contact and changes

Use the business contact shown on the contact page for privacy questions. Before accepting commercial orders, the responsible seller, operational retention policy and applicable terms must be published and approved.
