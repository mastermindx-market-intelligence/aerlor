FROM YOUR SELECTION TO YOUR ORDER

Every step.
No surprises.

  1. Select products and quantities

    Use your project bag to compare the collection and build a room set.

  2. Confirm destination and requirements

    Electrical version, intended placement, kit contents and timing affect the final proposal.

  3. Review a written price

    Currency, unit prices, volume basis, tax, shipping and applicable terms must be stated.

  4. Use secure checkout when eligible

    Only released, priced configurations can proceed to payment. Card details are entered on the payment provider’s checkout, not in an AERLOR form.
