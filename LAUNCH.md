# AERLOR / Commercial launch gates

The current website is a public design preview, not an operational sales channel. Public hosting does not mean that product claims, fulfillment, or lead handling are ready.

## Before outreach drives hotel purchasers to the site

- Confirm the brand name and trademark position; register the chosen domain separately. Do not use a registered-trademark symbol without a valid registration.
- Select the real production candidates. Replace the original concept visuals with approved product photography/renderings matching the actual units.
- Verify exact models, materials, electrical approval coverage, market/voltage variants, warranty and service policies. Publish measurable performance claims only with the supporting evidence.
- Approve actual wholesale pricing, minimum orders, sample terms and lead times. Do not use fabricated reference prices or customer endorsements.
- Establish a verified business contact identity and a real lead endpoint. Until then the private planner only downloads a local text brief and must not claim to send anything.
- Implement server-side validation, abuse protection, delivery monitoring, retention/deletion policies, and any consent requirements before accepting personal data. Add end-to-end receipt/error tests against that endpoint.
- Replace the preview privacy notice with the operational policy and appropriate company/contact details; review outreach compliance for each market separately.
- Configure the approved custom domain and HTTPS. Update base paths, sharing metadata and the 404 page. Remove `noindex` and the robots restriction only after the commercial content is approved.
- Complete manual screen-reader, mobile-device and touch review. Confirm real device performance, lighting/finish rendering, font-loading fallback and translated content where applicable.
- Keep the completed native Paper file and deployed implementation aligned as real product specifications replace the conceptual design.

## What is already implemented

The responsive collection experience, original product/architecture visuals, progressive interactive 3D, product detail dialogs, private pilot-brief builder, functional QA suite, pinned build/deployment workflow, native Paper website and interaction boards, exported design tokens, JSX references, screenshots, and a policy-gated Pages release path.

## Scope boundaries

No domains or paid services were purchased. No manufacturers, hotels, or external leads were contacted. No emails are sent by the website. No customer list, business credentials, private device paths, or account tokens belong in the repository.
