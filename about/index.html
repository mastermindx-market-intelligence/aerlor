THE AERLOR PERSPECTIVE

Hospitality is personal.
The essentials should be too.

We are building a collection around the moments guests bring with them: a morning routine, a final check in the mirror, the preparations for a night worth remembering.

DESIGN STUDY / AERLOR

Not more things.
More consideration.

A room can be beautifully designed and still ask a guest to improvise. AERLOR begins with the belief that everyday haircare and vanity tools deserve the same attention as the rest of the stay.

Our collection brings hairdryers, illuminated mirrors and styling tools into one design language, with Lite and Premium expressions for different rooms and service models.

The guest, first

Design around real routines, different preferences and the way a room is actually used.

The property, too

Consider the purchasing decision, engineering requirements, daily care and the service behind the object.

Evidence over embellishment

Separate the design ambition from verified product performance. A compelling story should never require invented proof.

Where we are today

AERLOR is a collection in development. The current visuals are original design studies. We are not claiming existing hotel installations, certified production models or ready-to-ship stock.
