Frequently asked questions.

Can I place a paid order now?+

Not yet. Online purchasing opens after the exact product, price, market configuration, merchant setup and supply terms are approved.

Are the technical specifications final?+

No. Product pages distinguish collection identity, proposed design details and technical information that still requires evidence.

Should styling tools be in every room?+

Not necessarily. A managed lending program may be a better fit. Heated tools require exact-model approval and a documented handling procedure before guest use.

Does a project inquiry subscribe me to marketing?+

No. The inquiry is for the conversation you request. It does not enrol you in promotional email.
